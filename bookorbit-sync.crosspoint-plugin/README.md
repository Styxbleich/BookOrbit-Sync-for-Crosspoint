BookOrbit Sync

Sends your reading sessions to your own BookOrbit server and syncs highlights
both ways. Made with AI, may contain errors.

How it works
Reading sessions are recorded on the reader as you read. They are sent to
BookOrbit the next time the reader goes online, for example when you use
Sync progress. Nothing to press.

Highlights sync both ways from the web interface, below: the reader's go to
BookOrbit, and BookOrbit's can be added to the reader.

Using the web interface
1. On the reader, open File Transfer and browse to the address it shows,
   from a device on the same network.
2. Click the Plugins tab and open the BookOrbit Sync card.

In the card:
- Server, KOReader sync login and device name: enter them, then Test
  connection and Save.
- Also send at sleep: sends sessions when the reader sleeps.
- Highlights: pick a book from your recent books, then
  Check book (confirms BookOrbit has it and compares highlights). Then
  add BookOrbit's to the reader, or send the reader's to BookOrbit, or
  delete ones BookOrbit lacks from the reader.
